Frequently Asked Questions

Is VIP Darshan included in the packages?
Yes! The VIP package includes priority darshan assistance at all major temples, meaning you skip long queues and get a personal, peaceful darshan experience.
Are meals included in the tour packages?
Yes. The Normal Package includes lunch and dinner. The VIP Package includes all three meals — breakfast, lunch and dinner. The Boat Tour includes evening lunch and tea. All meals are authentic vegetarian.
Can families and senior citizens book?
Absolutely. All packages are family-friendly and designed for pilgrims of all ages. We take special care to ensure comfort for senior citizens with appropriate transport and pacing.
How do I confirm my booking?
Simply WhatsApp us with your preferred package and travel date. A Rs.99 pre-booking charge confirms your slot. Remaining payment is due before the tour starts.
What is your cancellation and refund policy?
The Rs.99 booking charge is non-refundable. Refund eligibility depends on how far in advance you cancel. Last-minute cancellations and no-shows are non-refundable. Refunds process in 5–7 working days.
Is railway station pickup included?
Yes, all tour packages include pickup and drop at Chitrakoot/Karwi railway station, making your arrival and departure completely hassle-free.
